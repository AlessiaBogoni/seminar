**2. What question is the paper addressing?**

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-slate-300">
  <ul>
    <li>Should be obvious from title/abstract or first paragraphs</li>
    <li class="text-blue-400 font-semibold mt-1">&rarr; If not, indicative of a bad paper</li>
    <li>Identify the main question to put other parts of paper into context</li>
    <li class="text-blue-400 font-semibold mt-1">&rarr; Identify key parameter(s) and understand its (their) meaning</li>
    <li class="text-blue-400 font-semibold mt-1">&rarr; Check whether key parameters are statistically and economically significant</li>
  </ul>
</div>

# Five simple questions

<div class="text-xs text-slate-400 mb-3 space-y-1">
  <div><strong>1. Who is (are) the author(s)?</strong></div>
  <div><strong>2. What question is the paper addressing?</strong></div>
</div>

<span class="font-bold text-slate-100">3. What method is the paper using to answer the question?</span>

<div class="mt-3 space-y-3 text-slate-300 text-xs">
  <div>
    <strong>Is it a theoretical paper?</strong>
    <ul class="pl-4 mt-1 space-y-1 text-slate-400">
      <li>• Internal validity (hopefully) confirmed by editorial process</li>
      <li>• Analyze if assumptions valid/reasonable for the question of interest</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Is it an empirical study?</strong>
    <ul class="pl-4 mt-1 space-y-1 text-slate-400">
      <li>• Understand empirical strategy (set-up, data, identification strategy)</li>
      <li>• Identify key parameter(s) and understand its (their) meaning</li>
      <li>• Check whether key parameters are statistically and economically significant</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Is it a meta study / literature review?</strong>
    <ul class="pl-4 mt-1 space-y-1 text-slate-400">
      <li>• Identify those studies considered as seminal by the authors</li>
      <li>• Check if there is a consensus in the literature</li>
      <li>• Use study as reference tool</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

# Five simple questions

<div class="text-xs text-slate-400 mb-3 space-y-1">
  <div><strong>1. Who is (are) the author(s)?</strong></div>
  <div><strong>2. What question is the paper addressing?</strong></div>
  <div><strong>3. What method is the paper using to answer the question?</strong></div>
</div>

**4. What are the paper's results and (policy) conclusions?**

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-slate-300">
  <ul>
    <li>Assess how the conclusions are supported by the analysis</li>
    <li>Search for "robustness checks" that corroborate the authors findings</li>
  </ul>
</div>

# Five simple questions

<div class="text-xs text-slate-400 mb-3 space-y-1">
  <div><strong>1. Who is (are) the author(s)?</strong></div>

</div>

**5. How does the paper fit into the broader literature?**

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-slate-300">
  <ul>
    <li>Assess whether results are in line or contrary to previous work (applied work and theory)</li>
    <li>If results conflict, identify reasons for different findings</li>
  </ul>
</div>
